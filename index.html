Who we are

A global Omani company for AI & robotics

Established in 2018 in the Sultanate of Oman, AITECHOM combines high academic expertise with rich, diverse applied skills to deliver innovative solutions in robotic systems and AI-based big-data analysis.

We seek continuous research and development — attracting innovators locally and internationally, embracing and supporting them, and attracting investment so their ideas become reality.
